# VitaFit: Stay Consistent

A product management case study exploring how a fitness app could help inconsistent existing users return after missed workouts and build a more regular routine.

## Problem Hypothesis

Some existing users may begin with motivation but struggle to maintain a regular workout routine. Declining motivation and weak instructor connection are treated as hypotheses that require validation, not established VitaFit findings.

## Target Users

Existing VitaFit users who have started working out but are not yet consistent.

New users and already-consistent users are outside the scope of this case study.

## Proposed Solution

The proposed experience combines two feature groups:

### 1. Gamification and Rewards

- Weekly workout goals and streaks
- A short Recovery Workout after a missed workout
- An earned Comeback Pass that can protect a streak
- No points, coins, badges or reward marketplace in the MVP

### 2. Community Challenges

- A cooperative 100,000-step challenge for teams of three
- Invite friends and family or get matched with other users
- Contextual instructor encouragement
- No leaderboard or individual ranking

## Success Metric

The primary metric is the **7-Day Repeat Workout Rate**.

The proposed test compares the current experience with the Stay Consistent experience over four weeks.

Success requires:

- At least a 10% relative lift
- No agreed guardrail breach
- A follow-up experiment to identify which feature contributed to any observed improvement

## Evidence Boundary

This case study is based on secondary research and competitive analysis. No primary VitaFit user interviews, behavioural baseline or live experiment results were available.

The work proposes what to test. It does not claim validated impact or launch results.
